# CIA Triad Analysis Report

## Introduction

The CIA Triad is a fundamental model of information security. It consists of three core principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

This report analyzes five real-world cybersecurity incidents and identifies which CIA Triad principles were affected in each incident.

---

## 1. Yahoo Data Breach

### Incident

Yahoo experienced major data breaches that affected user accounts. The incidents involved unauthorized access to account-related information.

### Year

2013–2014

### CIA Principle Affected

**Confidentiality**

### Analysis

The incident primarily affected confidentiality because unauthorized individuals gained access to user information. Confidentiality requires sensitive information to be accessible only to authorized users.

### Impact

The exposure of user information created privacy and security risks for affected users.

---

## 2. Equifax Data Breach

### Incident

Equifax, a major credit reporting company, experienced a cybersecurity breach in 2017. Attackers gained unauthorized access to sensitive personal information.

### Year

2017

### CIA Principle Affected

**Confidentiality**

### Analysis

The primary failure was confidentiality because sensitive personal information was accessed by unauthorized individuals.

### Impact

The incident exposed personal information and created potential risks of identity theft and fraud for affected individuals.

---

## 3. SolarWinds Cyberattack

### Incident

The SolarWinds cyberattack involved the compromise of the company's software build process. Attackers inserted malicious code into legitimate software updates that were distributed to customers.

### Year

2020

### CIA Principle Affected

**Integrity**

### Analysis

The incident primarily affected integrity because legitimate software updates were modified before being distributed. Integrity requires information and software to remain accurate and protected from unauthorized modification.

### Impact

Organizations that installed the compromised updates could have their systems exposed to attackers.

---

## 4. WannaCry Ransomware Attack

### Incident

The WannaCry ransomware attack affected computers and organizations around the world. The ransomware prevented users from accessing files and systems.

### Year

2017

### CIA Principle Affected

**Availability**

### Analysis

The incident primarily affected availability because users could not access their files and systems normally.

### Impact

The attack disrupted organizations and prevented affected users from accessing important resources.

---

## 5. Dyn DDoS Attack

### Incident

Dyn, a major DNS service provider, was targeted by a large distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack.

### Year

2016

### CIA Principle Affected

**Availability**

### Analysis

The incident primarily affected availability because the attack disrupted Dyn's DNS services, making several internet services difficult or impossible for users to access.

### Impact

The disruption affected access to multiple popular websites and online services.

---

# CIA Triad Classification Summary

| Incident | Year | Primary CIA Principle | Main Failure |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Yahoo Data Breach | 2013–2014 | Confidentiality | Unauthorized access to user information |
| Equifax Data Breach | 2017 | Confidentiality | Exposure of sensitive personal information |
| SolarWinds Cyberattack | 2020 | Integrity | Unauthorized modification of software updates |
| WannaCry Ransomware Attack | 2017 | Availability | Users could not access files and systems |
| Dyn DDoS Attack | 2016 | Availability | Disruption of internet services |

---

# Interview Questions

## 1. What is Confidentiality?

Confidentiality means protecting information from unauthorized access or disclosure. It ensures that only authorized users can access sensitive information.

## 2. What is Integrity?

Integrity means maintaining the accuracy and completeness of information and protecting it from unauthorized modification or destruction.

## 3. What is Availability?

Availability means ensuring that information, systems, and services are accessible to authorized users whenever they are required.

---

# Conclusion

The five real-world incidents demonstrate how different cybersecurity attacks can affect different principles of the CIA Triad.

Data breaches primarily affect confidentiality, unauthorized modification can affect integrity, and service disruptions can affect availability.

Understanding the CIA Triad helps organizations identify security risks and implement appropriate security controls to protect information and systems.
